# 🩺 AURA Health Companion

**AURA Health Companion** is a smart healthcare application that integrates with smartwatches and AI technologies to provide complete health monitoring, medicine management, mental support, nutrition tracking, and preventive insights — all in one platform.

---

## 🌟 Features Overview

### 🕒 Watch Integration

- Collects vital data from the smartwatch such as:
  - Heartbeats, Oxygen ratio, Sleep quality, Calories count, etc.
- Uses user data (weight, height) to build predictive models for:
  - Stroke, Heart failure, Mental health issues, and more.

---

### 💊 Medicine Management

- Users can **add medicines**, and the app checks for **drug conflicts** automatically.
- If there’s a conflict, the app shows **which medicines conflict**.
- Supports **dose adjustment checks** to prevent dangerous interactions.
- Displays **medicine details**: usage frequency, dose, and additional notes.
- Visualizes **medicine strips** showing remaining capsules.
- Adds **realistic animation** when taking a capsule.
- **Conflict checker** for any medicine combinations.
- **Reminders** for taking medicines and notifications when they’re about to run out.
- Suggests **alternative medicines** with the same active ingredient (المادة الفعالة) and ensures safety.
- Allows **medicine ordering** with optional prescription upload (روشتة).

---

### 🧠 Mental Health Module

- Collects data via:
  - Simple questions (MCQs, True/False)
  - Smartwatch insights (movement, sleep, walking)
- Predicts:
  - Burnout, Stress, and related conditions.
- Provides:
  - **Exercises**, **videos**, and **podcasts** for recovery.
- Includes an **AI Chatbot** for mental support, doctor suggestions, and treatment guidance.
- **Quran-based therapy**:
  - Suggests verses, explanations, recitations, and supplications suitable for the user’s emotional state.

---

### 🍎 Nutrition & Diet

- User enters:
  - Weight, height, and chronic diseases (e.g., diabetes, high blood pressure).
- The app generates a **personalized diet plan**.
- Supports **intermittent fasting** tracking (الصيام المتقطع).
- Suggests **meals** based on dietary goals or fasting status.
- Uses **Agile tracking** to monitor user progress.

---

### 🛡️ Preventive Health Insights

- Tracks health improvements (e.g., weight loss, better sleep).
- Shows **disease prevention statistics**:
  - Example: “You prevented diabetes by 50% through increased movement.”

---

### 🏋️‍♂️ AI Fitness Coach

- User selects training type:
  - **Home**, **Gym**, **Push-Pull-Leg**, **Pro Split**, etc.
- Chooses a goal:
  - **Lose weight**, **Build muscle**, **Increase fitness**.
- App generates **custom training plans** using AI.
- AI checks the **correctness of exercises via camera**, acting as a virtual coach.

---

### 🚑 Emergency Assistant

- Detects **critical health anomalies**:
  - Low oxygen level, irregular heart rate, stroke symptoms, etc.
- Automatically sends **location and diagnosis** to emergency contact.
- Displays **popup recommendation** to call an ambulance.

---

### 🧬 Genetic Health Insights

- Users can upload **DNA analysis reports**.
- System predicts **potential diseases** (e.g., diabetes, cancer, high blood pressure).
- Provides **risk reduction guidance** and lifestyle tips.

---

### 👥 Community & Gamification

- Built-in **social platform** for users to support each other.
- Fun **challenges** like “Walk 10,000 steps in a day”.
- **Point system and leaderboard** — top 3 users receive offers and rewards.

---

### ⏰ Health Reminders

- Smart reminders for:
  - Drinking water, walking, exercising, sleeping, praying, etc.

---

### 🤖 AI Chatbot

- An all-in-one virtual assistant that supports:
  - Mental health guidance
  - Medicine information
  - Training plans
  - Nutrition advice
  - And more...

---

## 🧩 Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Flutter
- **Backend:** Supabase
- **Machine Learning Models:** TensorFlow / PyTorch (for predictions)
- **AI Chatbot:** OpenAI API / LLM integration
- **Database:** PostgreSQL (via Supabase)

---

## 📱 Vision

> _To create a holistic digital health ecosystem that empowers individuals to monitor, manage, and improve their health with AI-driven insights and compassionate technology._
